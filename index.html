🗺️ How to use the map

Use the location dropdown and zoom slider to focus on areas of interest. Circle size reflects seating capacity and color shows seating type (indoor/outdoor). Zoom in to inspect dense clusters and street contexts.

⭐ How to use the scatterplot

Filter by cuisine to compare restaurants. Read left→right for review volume (log scale) and bottom→top for rating quality. Larger points indicate higher price level. Look for top‑right clusters for popular, high‑rated venues.

📌 Quick facts: Cuisines

• Italian, Japanese, Thai, and Modern Australian are among the most represented cuisines in the CBD.
• Desserts and cafés often earn high ratings with moderate review counts—great for quick wins.

🧠 Strategic Intelligence

🏆 Market Dominance

NSW commands market leadership with 36% of national businesses, 30% of employment, and 32% of revenue. Victoria maintains strong second position, creating a duopoly in the eastern market.

📈 Profitability Analysis

Catering services achieve superior margins (5.3% vs 3.8%) through operational efficiency, while cafes & restaurants leverage volume through diversified revenue streams and on-premises dining.

💼 Economic Impact

The hospitality sector generates $13.7B in revenue and employs nearly 200,000 Australians, representing 1.5% of national employment and demonstrating significant economic contribution.

🧩 Turnover insights

📈 Annual vs monthly momentum

Annual change (line) captures the broader trend, while monthly change (bars) shows short‑term volatility. Sustained positives above the zero line signal underlying strength.

🗓️ Seasonality and quarters

Quarterly ticks highlight Nov–Dec demand peaks and post‑holiday soft patches. Compare peaks across years to see how quickly conditions normalise after shocks.

🛒 Shift to online

Online share is consistently higher for Non‑Food than Food. Spikes align with events and holidays; rising baselines suggest a structural shift toward e‑commerce.
