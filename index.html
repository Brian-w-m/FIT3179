🏆 Market Dominance

NSW commands market leadership with 36% of national businesses, 30% of employment, and 32% of revenue. Victoria maintains strong second position, creating a duopoly in the eastern market.

📈 Profitability Analysis

Catering services achieve superior margins (5.3% vs 3.8%) through operational efficiency, while cafes & restaurants leverage volume through diversified revenue streams and on-premises dining.

💼 Economic Impact

The hospitality sector generates $13.7B in revenue and employs nearly 200,000 Australians, representing 1.5% of national employment and demonstrating significant economic contribution.
